behavior adapted from MAME, with an independent JavaScript decoder and frontend.

The source template contains no TI firmware. A privately packaged copy may
contain owner-supplied ROMs in embedded-roms. The diagnostic is original code.
Timing follows instruction cycles at 4 MHz / 2. The custom slow-memory clock
divider and DOCK-BUS peripherals are not implemented. LCD 5x7 glyphs below are
an approximation; an optional HD44780 A00 character ROM overrides them.
